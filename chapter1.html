✦ Chapter 1: Mindset Rewiring

You must have a strong mindset to take the first step and read this guide. Congratulations!! You are already a step ahead of those who haven’t.

Now, here are a few more things I would like to discuss.
If you are currently a new middle school student, great for you! Because middle school, is the place where we actually build our base for higher education. Since you have got the awareness now, you are already in control. This is the time when you truly begin to understand studying — not just as something you have to do, but something that can actually shape your life. And if you get to know these tips and hacks now, it will benefit you because by the time you reach high school, you will already be experienced and prepared with what's coming. It’s not that learning these tips today will make you a topper overnight.

The real change comes from experimenting, failing, trying again, and practising every single day until it becomes a habit. Not overnight but slowly. It will take months, it will take years, but it's never too late. As I told you earlier, just take small steps like reading this guide every single day, from now on, which makes a habit and will benefit you later. You may think that you will start from tomorrow fresh and be perfect.
Wrong, you can’t. The truth is, you'll end up frustrated and struggling every time you think so big at once. Don’t try to change everything at once. Instead, take small steps from now. One simple thing you will do daily — until it becomes as natural as brushing your teeth. Just one habit, for example, reading this guide. Instead of watching reels, you can just take 5-10 minutes to read this guide before you scroll. I’m not telling you to skip that forever, even though I scroll too, and everyone does, even toppers, but they know how to prioritise other tasks with scrolling (I'll be teaching this to you later in the following chapters).

So the main point is,
You may not succeed in your next exams.
You may not be a topper in your next exam.
And that’s okay, it's part of growth.

I won't be assuring you with Pinterest-perfect motivation, which is flashy but not useful at all. And nobody can succeed in such a short time. It will take years to convert your mindset, change your habits into that one, so take patience, and don’t chase perfection. If you start your journey today, right now, with the smallest habit, you are already ahead of many years, and already a part of your successful life now. Just clear and small goals. And one day will come when you'll be looking back at your hard work and thanking your past self. It's still possible. Just be confident and believe you can do this. You will not just achieve your goal, you'll be the type of person you want to be--- a topper, which will help you achieve further what you want to do in your future life. Remember it's progress, not perfection. Just a little every day can save you from a lot of stress before the exam/deadline. Change this mindset, and you are already a lot of steps ahead!!! Don’t be afraid that you'll be boring and your life will be boring with discipline. It won't be until you clear your mind of those useless thoughts. Discipline is not studying 12 hours a day, it's not having no friends, it's not about having no social life. If you think this way, it’s a lie. Have you ever talked to a topper? I was too afraid to do so, because our class topper was a really cold and calm one and didn’t alk much. But when I became a little close friends with her, my whole pov towards her changed. She was very interesting, and she even talked about brainrots and aura farming. (fr ik LOL). So your life won't be boring, don’t worry about it. You can join as many fandoms as you want, continue gaming, pursue your hobby, and almost do everything you want, and most of all be yourself and have funn!!! It's all about time management, which we are gonna learn in the next chapter.
