✦ Chapter 2: Time Management and Timetables ✦

Yes, time management is the first thing you should do! Because if you don’t know what time you will study or scroll or do anything else, you cannot be consistent with anything. Make a fixed schedule. And no, don’t make a very unrealistic and too-perfect timetable which you won't be able to follow after 2 days.

First, for now, don’t make a timetable — just observe the tasks you usually do on your school days. After observing, write them. Don’t give timestamps (except waking up times and study times) because those are the traps; these make your timetable too perfect.

Write the part of the day when you are doing the tasks. This helps you overlook your tasks. I also did it the same way, trust me! Just write the things you do order by order in a list. Be honest with yourself, nothing to lie about. Add as many detailed tasks as you can.

Let’s assume your present routine looks like this:

✨ Part of the Day 📌 Task
Morning (7 am) Wake up
Morning Scroll a little
Morning Pack bag (maybe the night before)
Morning Breakfast
Morning Get dressed
Morning Leave for school
Afternoon Come back from school
Afternoon Eat lunch
Afternoon Scroll again
Evening Tuition/study session 1
Evening More tuition/study session 2
Night Come back from tuition & chill/break
Night (on tuition days) Self study/homeworks
Night (optional) Hobbies/more scrolling

Now you can identify which parts need to change and which habits to add.

Not only change the habits, but also their placement. Like you don’t have to scroll right after waking up. You can check your phone later, like before going to school — no scrolling, just a little notification check, messaging back people, or even sending something important.

You can give hobbies more time and create a peaceful morning and night routine as well. This saves you from guilt, as well as managing your morning well!

(Watching your phone in the morning right after waking up is also harmful — I’ll explain it in future chapters.)

Now your updated routine might look like this:

✨ Part of the Day 📌 Task
Morning (7 am) Wake up + stretch + drink water
Morning Pack bag (maybe the night before)
Morning Breakfast
Morning Get dressed + check phone
Morning Leave for school
Afternoon Come back from school
Afternoon Eat lunch
Afternoon Scroll + enjoy!
Afternoon Do hobbies, go out and touch grass no like fr :)
Evening Tuition/study session 1
Evening More tuition/study session 2
Night Come back from tuition & chill/break
Night Chill + journal + night routine

Now you know how to build timetables suitable for your needs. Nothing complex, nothing too boring, right?

It includes hobbies, chill time, scrolling, journalling, and going out! What can be more fun than that?

And if you stick to your routine and complete your tasks and studying on time instead of procrastinating, this gives you more time to enjoy your life!

If you scroll the whole day saying you have a lot of studying and you’ll eventually do it later, well... you are going to end up cramming at the last minute with very low-quality results.

Good things take time. And time can be managed by a timetable.
